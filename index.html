Frequently Asked

Can I customise an order?
Absolutely! We craft bespoke pieces. Contact us via the inquiry form or WhatsApp to start your custom design.
What gold purity do you offer?
We work with 18K, 22K, and 24K gold, hallmarked and certified. For diamond pieces, we use GIA-certified stones.
Shipping & delivery time?
We ship within 3–5 business days via insured courier. International shipping is available – reach out for details.
Return / exchange policy?
We offer 7-day return on unused items with original packaging. Custom orders are non-returnable unless defective.
