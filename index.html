✦ FAQ

Custom orders – can I request a unique design?

Absolutely! We specialise in bespoke pieces. Contact us via the inquiry form or WhatsApp to start your custom design journey.

What is the gold purity of your jewellery?

All our gold pieces are 22k and 18k, hallmarked for authenticity. We provide purity certificates with every purchase.

Shipping & delivery – how long does it take?

We ship worldwide via insured courier. Domestic delivery 2–4 days, international 5–10 days. Tracking provided.

Return policy – what if I’m not satisfied?

We offer a 7-day return policy on unused items with original packaging. Custom orders are non-returnable.
